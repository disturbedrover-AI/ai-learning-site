n8n workflows are drawn as boxes joined by arrows: a trigger (like a new email),
then steps that transform data or call apps and AI models. The source code is
public under its own "Sustainable Use" licence, the free community edition can be
self-hosted, and n8n Cloud is a paid hosted service.
