## The short answer

Artificial intelligence (AI) is software that can perform tasks we usually
associate with human thinking: recognising images, understanding language,
making predictions, or making decisions.

## Where you already use it

- Spam filters in your email
- Recommendations on streaming and shopping sites
- Voice assistants and live captions

## What AI is not

AI doesn't "understand" the world the way people do. It finds patterns in
data and uses them to produce useful results.
