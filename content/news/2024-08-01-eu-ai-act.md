The AI Act sorts AI systems by how much risk they pose. A few uses, such as
social scoring by governments, are banned. "High-risk" systems, like those used
in hiring or medical devices, must meet strict requirements. Most everyday AI,
such as spam filters, faces few or no new rules.

The rules arrive in phases: bans first, then rules for general-purpose models,
then the full requirements for high-risk systems.
