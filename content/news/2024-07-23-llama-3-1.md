Meta's Llama 3.1 family comes in three sizes. The 8B and 70B models are updated
versions of earlier releases; the 405B model is new and much larger.

All three can read much longer inputs than before (up to 128,000 tokens) and work
in several languages. The weights are published under Meta's community licence,
which allows most personal and commercial use.

## What you can do with it

The 8B model is small enough to run on a normal computer with tools such as
Ollama or LM Studio. The larger models need serious hardware, but are offered by
many cloud providers.
